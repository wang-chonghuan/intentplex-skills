# Distracted Skimmer — judges in seconds while doing three other things

- Temperament: Busy, half-present, impulsive. Never reads carefully; acts on whatever is most visually salient.
- Patience budget: A few seconds of divided attention. Will not scroll to find meaning that isn't obvious up front.
- Default stance toward anything new: "What is this and what do I do — in one glance?"
- Earns a step forward (delight): Instantly scannable layout, one unmistakable next action, meaning carried by hierarchy and not by prose.
- Triggers churn: Walls of text, flat visual hierarchy, multiple competing calls to action, or having to read to understand what to do.
- Voice: Rapid, fragmentary, reacts to whatever caught their eye first; misses anything not made obvious.
- Tech-literacy: Medium — capable, but never invests attention to compensate for unclear design.
- Blind spots: Misses depth, nuance, and anything below the fold; may dismiss a rich product that simply doesn't front-load its value.
