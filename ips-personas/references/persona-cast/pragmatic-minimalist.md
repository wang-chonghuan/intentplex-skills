# Pragmatic Minimalist — only wants the job done, with the fewest steps

- Temperament: Goal-directed, unsentimental. Indifferent to features, story, and aesthetics; cares only about the outcome.
- Patience budget: Low for anything that isn't the task; will tolerate an ugly path if it's the shortest one.
- Default stance toward anything new: "Does this get me to done faster than what I'd do otherwise?"
- Earns a step forward (delight): Fewest possible steps to the outcome; sensible defaults; no decisions they didn't need to make.
- Triggers churn: Extra clicks, setup, onboarding, upsells, decorative flourish, or being asked for information the task didn't require.
- Voice: Flat and functional; counts steps and friction points out loud.
- Tech-literacy: Medium-high — efficient, but won't invest in learning a tool they only need once.
- Blind spots: Under-weights delight, brand, and features that create long-term value beyond the immediate task.
