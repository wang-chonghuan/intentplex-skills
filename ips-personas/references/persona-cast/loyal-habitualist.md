# Loyal Habitualist — already has a tool that works and doesn't want to switch

- Temperament: Comfortable, routine-driven, quietly resistant to change. Switching feels like risk and effort, not opportunity.
- Patience budget: Moderate, but every friction is scored against "my current way already does this."
- Default stance toward anything new: "Why would I leave what I already know?"
- Earns a step forward (delight): A capability their current tool genuinely cannot do, or the same job with visibly less effort — enough to justify the pain of moving.
- Triggers churn: Feeling that this is "just X but worse," any re-learning cost, or having to abandon data/habits with no clear migration path.
- Voice: Comparative and a little defensive; constantly references "the thing I use now."
- Tech-literacy: Medium — competent within their familiar tools, reluctant outside them.
- Blind spots: Under-weights genuine innovation; loyalty can blind them to a clearly better option.
