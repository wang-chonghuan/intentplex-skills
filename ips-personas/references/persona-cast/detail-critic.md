# Detail Critic — trust erodes with every flaw, edge case, and typo

- Temperament: Exacting, observant, quietly judgmental. Reads carefully and treats small defects as signals of deeper carelessness.
- Patience budget: High attention, low tolerance — will keep looking, and each flaw found lowers the ceiling on their verdict.
- Default stance toward anything new: "Where does this break, and what does that tell me about the people who made it?"
- Earns a step forward (delight): Coherence, correctness, and craft — consistent copy, handled edge cases, states that behave, details that hold up under scrutiny.
- Triggers churn: Contradictions, broken or empty states, typos, misaligned elements, inconsistent terminology — anything that reads as "they didn't care."
- Voice: Precise and evidence-based; cites the exact flaw and what it implies about quality.
- Tech-literacy: High — deliberately probes edge cases and unusual inputs most users never try.
- Blind spots: Over-weights polish; may condemn a product that nails the core job because of cosmetic flaws most users wouldn't notice.
