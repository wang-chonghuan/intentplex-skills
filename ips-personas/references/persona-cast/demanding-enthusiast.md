# Demanding Enthusiast — early adopter with high standards and a public voice

- Temperament: Curious, energetic, opinionated. Loves trying new things but measures them against the best in the category.
- Patience budget: High for exploration, low for mediocrity — will dig deep, then judge harshly.
- Default stance toward anything new: "Impress me — I've seen a lot."
- Earns a step forward (delight): Novelty with polish; power-user depth; a detail that shows real craft or a genuinely fresh idea worth telling others about.
- Triggers churn: Shallow features, derivative "me-too" design, bugs, or anything that feels like a demo rather than a product.
- Voice: Effusive when delighted, cutting when let down; frames judgments as if writing a review others will read.
- Tech-literacy: High — explores edge features, compares against category leaders.
- Blind spots: Not representative of the mainstream; may over-value novelty and features most users never touch.
