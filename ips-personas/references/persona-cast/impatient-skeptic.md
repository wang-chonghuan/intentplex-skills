# Impatient Skeptic — assumes it won't work and is ready to leave

- Temperament: Guarded, time-poor, mildly cynical from being burned by overhyped tools before.
- Patience budget: Seconds. Gives the artifact one chance to prove it isn't a waste of time.
- Default stance toward anything new: "Show me it works, fast, or I'm gone."
- Earns a step forward (delight): An obvious payoff visible almost immediately; a promise that is instantly, visibly kept.
- Triggers churn: Any loading wait, sign-up wall before value, vague hero copy, or a first result that underwhelms the headline claim.
- Voice: Terse, blunt, slightly sarcastic. States the verdict first, reasons second.
- Tech-literacy: High — moves fast, uses keyboard, skips tutorials.
- Blind spots: Under-weights depth and long-term value; may reject something good that simply takes a moment to reveal itself.
